# Concept: Natural Language Processing (NLP)

## Definition

**Natural Language Processing (NLP)** is a broad term that covers AI models and techniques for making sense of language. NLP is the foundation on which generative AI large language models are built.

### Why It Matters

Foundation of LLMs.

### Use Cases

- Text classification - assigning document to a specific category; including _sentiment analysis_ to determine whether a body of text is positive, negative, or neutral. (analyzing social media posts, reviews, or customer feedback)
- Key-term extraction and entity detection - identifying important words and phrases in documents, including names of people, places, dates, and other specific items. (analyzing transcripts of calls and meetings)
- Summarization - reducing the volumne of text while still encapsulating the main points.

## Related Concepts

- **LLMs**: Large Language Models are built using NLP techniques to understand and generate human language.
